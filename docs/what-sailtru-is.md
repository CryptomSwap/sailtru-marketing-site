# What SailTrue is, and what it is looking to be

Canonical identity, positioning, and **mobile homepage composition**. Tom’s words. Read this before copy, design, competitive work, or any Mike job on SailTrue / sailtru.com.

This is the company. It is not a sprint plan and it does not authorize a merge or a deploy.

Public site today: **SailTru**, [sailtru.com](https://sailtru.com), this repo. Company name: **SailTrue**.

---

## What it is

SailTrue is a **business intelligence and AI solutions** company. It helps companies understand where they can improve, what opportunities matter most, and how to act on them.

Its **platform** provides evidence-backed business intelligence across a company’s digital presence, market, competitors, and growth opportunities.

On top of that, SailTrue **builds bespoke AI solutions**: custom automations, specialized agents, and full AI agent teams designed around a company’s workflows.

It is **not just an analytics platform** and **not just an AI agency**.

## What it is looking to be

The layer that connects business intelligence with AI-powered execution.

```
Observe → Understand → Prioritize → Recommend → Build → Monitor → repeat
```

**Question to protect:**

> What should this business improve next — and can we implement it?

SEO, GEO, AI visibility, competitor intelligence, automation and agent systems are **capabilities inside that system**. They are not the identity. Do not lead with `SEO · GEO · Custom AI`.

Narrative: **We found the opportunity → here is how we can implement it.** Not: **Meet your AI workforce.**

Agents, automations, and bespoke multi-agent teams appear only after Prioritize / Recommend. They are implementations, not a SaaS catalog.

## Two doors

| Door | For | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Observe** | Analyze the business and identify what should improve | See what to improve / Analyze my business |
| **Build** | Already knows it needs a system, automation, or AI implementation | Build with SailTrue / Talk to us |

No fake SaaS free-trial. Diagnosis and bespoke implementation are different engagement paths.

## Competitive set (short)

No 1:1 competitor. Closest strategic peer: **Relevance AI** (execution half — they ask “what can we automate?”). Others are layers: CrewAI, Crayon, Klue, Similarweb, Profound, Peec/Otterly, Deloitte. The real competitor is a **stack**. Borrow mobile *discipline* (quiet chrome, one job). Do not borrow identity.

## Scan product (honest)

Target output of Observe:

1. Ranked things the business should improve
2. Evidence supporting each conclusion
3. Why it matters
4. Whether SailTrue can implement it
5. What implementation could look like

Build the frontend for that loop. Do not fake a ranked list if the backend is not there yet. Do not present the scan as a generic SEO/GEO/Similarweb checker.

## Mobile homepage composition (`< 980px`, validate `390 × 844`)

Desktop stays as it is unless a change is explicitly shared. Do not squeeze the desktop header. Priority: **identity → hierarchy → flow → proof → polish.** No new visual system.

### P0 — Header

Only: wordmark + hamburger. Locale and Diagnosis live in the drawer. Quiet.

### P0 — Hero (first viewport)

**Peec structure + Profound motion.** Header, H1, one quiet sentence, oval Run scan, secondary Build. Field wash + dots. No mock product chrome in the first viewport.

- **H1:** What should your business improve next? (word blur on load — Profound craft)
- **Supporting:** SailTrue studies your business, market and digital presence, prioritizes what matters, and helps implement the right solution. Short line-length, muted — does not compete with H1.
- **Primary:** Oval URL + Run scan → (`#observe`). Login with host until ranked output exists.
- **Secondary:** Quiet Build with SailTrue / Talk to us (Calendly). Two doors, no free-trial lie.
- **Not in ATF:** Fake Observe shell, Live session, process sidebar, WhatsApp, desktop diagram.

### P0 — Observe (below the fold)

Scroll-resolved blur statement + short lede. No mock product chrome / analysis frame on mobile — type and loop carry the story.

- Label: `01 — Observe`
- Blur statement → lede → loop
- Entry into the intelligence loop.

### P1 — Loop

Kill intel-fallback boards / chips / pin-scroll / WebGL under 980px. Six stages, number + one-word title + 1–2 sentences. Progression, not a card grid. No Sigma / Twin / Judgment vocabulary.

| Stage | Intent |
| --- | --- |
| Observe | Gather evidence about the business, website, market and competitive environment. |
| Understand | Turn fragmented signals into a reliable picture of what is happening. |
| Prioritize | Determine what matters most and what should be improved next. |
| Recommend | Translate the evidence into clear, defensible actions. |
| Build | Implement the solution — including bespoke AI systems, automations and agent teams where appropriate. |
| Monitor | Measure whether the intervention actually improved the business. |

### P1 — Proof early

Laba within ~two scrolls: **+275% traffic**. No invented metrics. Restrained. Message: SailTrue does not only analyze. It implements and measures.

### P1 — Execution after intelligence

After Prioritize / Recommend: research, competitive monitoring, lead qualification, operations, customer-support, internal knowledge agents; workflow automations; **bespoke agent teams around actual workflows**.

### P1 — Monitor

Close the loop on the page: we don’t stop when something ships. Monitor what changed, whether it worked, and what should improve next.

WhatsApp FAB on the opening (homepage) experience; desktop intelligence boards; pin-scroll/WebGL storytelling; floating scan capsule in the hero; multiple hero CTAs; How we work hero control; locale chip; Begin Diagnosis chip; SEO · GEO · Custom AI rail.
