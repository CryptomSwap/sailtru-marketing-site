## The current story

1. **Visibility Lighthouse:** track the business in search and AI answers (SEO/GEO).
2. **Competitor Bay:** compare its visibility and answer coverage with competitors.
3. **Opportunity Point:** identify a specific gap and a practical next action.
4. **Action House:** help create the missing answer and implement the improvement.
5. **Your AI Crew:** an optional capability—bespoke AI and agent teams take research,
   follow-ups, and reporting off the team's desk, with human review.
6. **Tracking:** keep reviewing visibility, competitors, enquiries, and time saved.

The continuous example is a missing answer to a buyer's pricing question. The
comparison and page are explicitly illustrative; they are not live measured data.
English/Hebrew hero, narrative, report, and final CTA use the same offer.
