# SailTru subpages: homepage language

**Taste source:** the live homepage (sailtru-com-en). Improve job — design-kit doesn't apply. No screenshot of contact; its note is from text structure only.

## What the homepage does

- **Ground:** cobalt field at the top — saturated blue gradient, faint orbit rings/dots on the right — running from header through hero and second band. A lavender fade leads into a pale, cool off-white ground holding every section below. The closing CTA is a white card with a soft shadow on that pale ground.
- **Accents:** cobalt for eyebrows, numerals, thick top rules on step columns, the filled button, the email underline. Cyan only on the italic hero word; everything else ink and slate grey.
- **Type, three voices:** condensed high-contrast serif for display (upright headings, italic sub-heads/quotes/row titles); monospace for eyebrows (small, tracked, uppercase, cobalt), numerals, nav, buttons, step labels; sans body, which becomes **Heebo** wherever text isn't display.
- **Density:** calm below the fold — eyebrow → display heading → one-line lede → content, hairline dividers between rows/sections, wide left-aligned container, generous vertical space. Work quote and CTA sit in a narrower centred column.
- **Hero:** headline left, signal-to-action diagram right. **Absent below the fold:** photos, icons, gradients on cards, coloured section backgrounds, metric tiles.

## Shared language (all four pages)

1. **Header:** the v2 header as on the homepage — logo, mono nav with current page in white, HE toggle, white-glass "Begin diagnosis" button, hairline beneath. Already on services, how-we-work, work. Contact must use the same one.
2. **Cobalt field hero:** same gradient and grain as the homepage, orbit rings and dots faint on the trailing side. No diagram, cards or illustration. Mono eyebrow, display serif headline in white, Heebo lede in pale blue-white. Taller than today's short band so it reads as a field, shorter than the homepage hero.
3. **Transition:** same lavender fade from the cobalt field into the pale ground.
4. **Section rhythm:** every section below the hero follows the homepage pattern — mono cobalt eyebrow, display heading, Heebo lede, content in the wide container, full-width hairline between sections. Numbered rows use the homepage territory-row form (mono numeral in its own column, mono eyebrow, italic serif title, Heebo line). Step sets use the homepage method form (columns under a thick cobalt rule, numeral plus mono label, Heebo line).
5. **Close:** each page ends with the homepage white CTA card (eyebrow, display heading, lede, cobalt filled button plus underlined mono secondary link). Use each page's existing strings. Don't add copy.
6. **Hebrew:** same components, mirrored — mono numeral column and orbit rings move to the opposite side.
7. **Motion:** add nothing beyond what the homepage's shared components already do. No new scroll or entrance effects.

**Not adding:** hero diagrams or screenshots on subpages, cyan outside the homepage, new icons, card grids, new metrics or figures, new copy, any library. Homepage, login, dashboard, message catalogs stay untouched.
