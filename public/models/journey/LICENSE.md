# Sailing yacht model

`sailing-yacht-8m.glb` is the “Sailing yacht, 8 m” model from the Innerscene 3D Parts Library.

## SailTru logo boat

`sailtru-logo-boat.glb` is an original model created specifically for SailTru from the supplied SailTru symbol. Its editable source is `sailtru-hero-boat.blend`, and its reproducible Blender modeling source is `scripts/build-sailtru-boat-blender.py`.

`scripts/generate-sailtru-logo-boat.mjs` is retained as the lightweight procedural prototype that preceded the Blender production asset.
