The promise: Follow a buyer from their first question to a business. SailTru tracks search and AI visibility, compares competitors, identifies opportunities and helps make the improvements.
The visual contract: one large beach bay, 4 → 8 → 12 berths, long journeys to distant competitors, and steady incoming/outgoing client traffic. Optional AI works inside the business; it is never represented by client boats.
