# SailTru — the client journey

Storyboard V2 · 29 October 2026 · Review before production changes

Follow a potential client from a buying question to the business they discover; then show how SailTru helps your business become easier to find, understand and engage with.

## The metaphor

| Visual | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Every boat | A potential client, including those arriving, leaving or considering competitors. |
| One blue boat | The buyer we follow throughout the story. Blue identifies the narrative focus. |
| A bay | A business. Your bay is one large crescent beach with a recognizable shoreline throughout. |
| Grey symbol above a boat | The discovery channel the buyer is using: ChatGPT, Claude, Google or Perplexity. It does not mean the boat is an AI agent. |
| Open water and routes | Discovery and consideration. Distance is narrative space, not a ranking or travel-time metric. |
| A berth | A place to engage: a useful destination page, enquiry or conversation. Docking is a visit, not necessarily a sale. |
| An expanding pier | Improvements to the experience and capacity to receive interested buyers. Illustrative, not guaranteed demand. |
| Work inside the shore-side office | Optional bespoke AI solutions and agent teams helping the business. These never become boats. |

Provider marks are monochrome grey in off-white medallions, with consistent visual weight. Introduce their meaning with a short legend. The badge faces the screen and follows its boat without rotating with the hull. Keep the hero buyer's ChatGPT badge consistent across the opening and return. Other buyers show other channels. **No branded destination islands:** ChatGPT is where the buyer asks, not a business they sail to purchase from.

## Six chapters

### 01 — Follow the buyer · 0–13% scroll

**Message:** Every journey starts with a question.

Start close to a single blue boat moving through open water, with distant bays on the horizon. A grey ChatGPT symbol floats above it. Follow it for several hull lengths, then open a readable conversation:

> Which payment provider is the best?

The answer can clarify that the right option depends on fees, integrations and supported markets. Hold the boat and conversation in the same composition. This is an illustrative buying scenario, not a fabricated live response or a real product recommendation.

**POV:** low three-quarter tracking shot → fixed conversation view. Understand the buyer before introducing SailTru.

### 02 — Travel to a discovered business · 13–31%

**Message:** Being an option comes before being chosen.

An illustrative answer offers Provider A and Provider B to compare. Your business is absent in this sample. The buyer follows one option along a continuous route to its bay. The destination grows naturally as the camera approaches.

Place the competitor approximately **4–6 main-bay widths away** in the eventual world, with substantial open water between shores. This is a composition target, not a metric measured from the static drawing. The storyboard compresses geography; the final travelling shot must cover the actual separation. Avoid adjacent bays in close harbour views.

**POV:** rise into a wide establishing view → travel beside the buyer → reveal the competitor on approach. Give the voyage a long scroll allocation. A visit is not a closed sale.

### 03 — Understand why you were missing · 31–47%

**Message:** SailTru makes the missing route visible.

Pull back into a market view. Carry the same question into a stable evidence card. Show the four channels, then one concrete observation: in this sample, a competitor explains fees and integrations while your business leaves those comparison questions unanswered.

SailTru tracks **SEO and GEO visibility**, compares relevant competitors, investigates differences and prioritizes useful improvements. This is one finding, not a claim that one content gap fully explains ranking or AI recommendations. Technical accessibility, content, evidence and the enquiry experience can all be part of the work.

**POV:** market context → close, still evidence card. Quiet the background while the card is read. No invented overall score.

### 04 — Improve the same business · 47–63%

**Message:** Build a business that is easier to discover and choose.

Return to your bay. Show the work first: a useful fees-and-integrations page, clear supporting information, improved technical accessibility and a relevant enquiry path. Then extend the pier from **4 to 8 berths**. Keep the shoreline, palm clusters and buildings recognizable.

Every visible change has a reason. Useful answers precede a new place for visitors to engage. The island should not merely accumulate decorations. Natural beach contours and a few carefully placed buildings matter more than object count.

**POV:** descend toward the bay → close on the implemented page → reveal the expanded pier. Hold the finished improvement.

### 05 — Revisit the buyer's journey · 63–82%

**Message:** A clearer route from discovery to your business.

Return to the same blue boat, ChatGPT badge and question. In a later illustrative answer, your business is an option to compare. Let the buyer read, then follow a substantial approach through open water, coastline and harbour entrance. They reach the useful page and a berth representing an enquiry opportunity.

Other buyers can keep going or explore another business. SailTru measures what changes; a single edit does not guarantee inclusion, a visit or a purchase. This depicts the intended journey, not a client-results claim.

**POV:** close to the buyer → tracking voyage → rising view of your bay as the destination. No teleporting from conversation to dock.

### 06 — A bay with momentum · 82–100%

**Message:** More room for the people who find you.

Your bay dominates the final view. Grow from **8 to 12 berths**, keeping some open so capacity and arrivals remain legible. Boats with all four grey marks arrive and depart on separate lanes. The competitor remains a small, distant landmark.

A quiet office inset introduces the independent optional offer: **bespoke AI solutions and agent teams**. Research briefs, follow-up drafts and reporting move into tailored workflows with visible human review. The desk clears; boats remain potential clients. AI work can be commissioned separately from SEO/GEO work.

End with two paths: **Improve your visibility** and **Build an AI solution**. The ongoing loop is track → compare → improve → measure. Review actual visibility, enquiry quality and, where relevant, time saved; background boat counts are not business metrics.

**POV:** settle into a wide elevated bay composition. Hold the final message and choices while ambient traffic continues.

## Motion plan for later implementation

- **Story motion follows scroll.** The hero buyer, camera, cards and berth upgrades have deterministic positions. Reverse scrolling reverses the story coherently.
- **Ambient traffic follows time.** Independent client boats arrive and depart while the visitor pauses. Start with 6–10 visible in the final desktop composition, fewer on mobile; stagger their trips.
- **Separate inbound and outbound lanes.** Boats ease into berths, pause briefly and turn along plausible curves. Never shrink them to nothing, disappear mid-water or reset in the foreground. Recycle only beyond all relevant camera views.
- **Longer means sustained travel and useful POV changes.** Use world separation, a moving coastline and an approach sequence. Camera tracking keeps the hero readable. Do not fake distance by stretching a decorative dotted line or making boats tiny.
- **Hold the message.** One dominant action, then a reading hold. Cards stay screen-aligned; camera travel never drags text around. Ambient traffic stays outside the reading area.
- **Quiet palette.** Warm off-white, dark grey ink and SailTru blue. Sand and water are neutral tints. Logos stay grey; blue identifies the current buyer, finding or action.
- **Stable badges.** Screen-facing, clamped readable size, light tether, minimal bobbing. No spin or abrupt pop. Keep badges out of reading cards; use the channel legend when marks alone are ambiguous.
- **Mobile.** Art above text, fewer boats and reduced lateral camera travel. Preserve all six narrative beats and readable questions.
- **Reduced motion.** Show complete still states with the same text and sequence. Disable ambient loops and travelling cameras.
